Title: First-run onboarding review
Written: August 13, 2026

Five fictional testers completed the setup flow. Four finished without help.
The median completion time was 2 minutes 36 seconds. The one tester who needed
help did not understand that the local model had to be running before the first
chat request.

Recommendation: keep onboarding below three minutes, test the local model as
part of setup, and give the person one button that explains and repairs common
local-model connection problems.
